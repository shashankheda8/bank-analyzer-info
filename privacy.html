Privacy Policy

Bank Statement Analyzer is a private tool used only by its owner. It accesses only the owner's own Gmail and Google Sheets through Google OAuth, solely to read bank statement emails and write a summary to the owner's own spreadsheet.

No data is collected from other users, shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising. Data stays within the owner's Google account and the owner's private automation.
